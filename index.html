FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

Are you licensed and insured plumbers?

Yes. Kitchener Plumbing Pros works under a valid plumbing licence with full insurance, and anything requiring a permit gets one filed with the municipality and inspected to the Ontario Building Code.

Do you handle plumbing emergencies in Kitchener?

Yes. Burst pipes, sewer backups, no water, and flooding get priority dispatch across the city, and our trucks carry the common parts so most emergencies get fixed on the first visit.

How fast can you replace a failed water heater?

Usually same day. Common tank sizes ride on the truck, so a Kitchener swap-out typically happens within hours of your call, rental units included.

My older Kitchener home has galvanized pipe. Is it worth repiping?

If taps run rusty and pressure drops when two fixtures open, yes. Galvanized rusts closed from the inside, and replacing it with PEX or copper restores pressure and ends the pinhole-leak cycle.

Can our basement second suite drains handle two households?

Often not without help. Suites push double the water through laterals laid for one family, so we camera the line and clear or upgrade it before the tenants move downstairs.

Is Kitchener water hard enough to need a softener?

It is. The city draws largely from groundwater aquifers, and most homes see heavy scale on fixtures and tanks. We test at the tap and size the softener to the real number.

How do you price plumbing work?

Quotes are free and written before anything starts. Whether the job is a toilet swap or a whole-house repipe, the price we agree on is the price you pay.

Which areas do you serve around Kitchener?

All of Kitchener plus Waterloo, Cambridge, Elmira, Ayr, New Hamburg, Baden, and the surrounding countryside of the Region of Waterloo.
